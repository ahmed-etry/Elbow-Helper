# Getting started

Your Discord roles determine which commands you can use. `/help` shows those
commands; choose one for its options, examples, and important restrictions.

## Your Clash accounts

One Discord member can have several linked Clash accounts. Those links let
recruitment, rosters, CWL, wars, records, and reports use the correct account.

[Recruiters and Core leadership](../reference/commands-and-permissions.md#access-groups)
manage links with `/account add`, `/account list`, and `/account remove`. If a
link is wrong or an account is missing, ask them to check it.

## Member commands

- `/help` shows what you can use or explains one command.
- `/ping` checks whether the bot is responding.
- `/plan` lets you share a base and ask for an attack plan that fits your army
  levels and preferred strategies.
- `/achievements` shows your progress and rewards.
- `/achievement leaderboard` ranks members by achievements earned.
- `/inventory` shows your coins and raffle ticket.
- `/economyinfo` explains coins, tickets, and rewards.
- `/transfer request` and `/transfer cancel` manage a family-clan transfer
  request.
- `/reactivate` returns you from hibernation.
