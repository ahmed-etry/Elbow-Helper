# Examinations and community automation

## Promotion examinations

Promotion tickets ask which clan the applicant is in, where they want to move,
and their Town Hall level when needed. When an exam is required, the bot
collects the applicant's availability, finds examiners with matching times, and
sends follow-ups when the request needs attention.

## Leadership news

Messages in the leadership news channel receive Publish and Dismiss controls.
Publishing copies the message text, member mentions, links, emoji, and up to
three attachments to the community news channel. Role mentions are removed.
Dismiss removes the controls without posting the message.
