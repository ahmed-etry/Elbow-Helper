# Achievements, events, access, and support

## Achievements and economy

`/achievements` shows completed achievements, progress toward unfinished ones,
and their coin rewards. `/achievement leaderboard` ranks the top 10 members
outside leadership by completed achievements. `/inventory` shows your coin
balance and whether you have this month's raffle ticket. `/economyinfo`
explains how coins, raffle tickets, and rewards work.

[Lead](../../reference/commands-and-permissions.md#access-groups) can award or
remove achievements.
[Lead Plus](../../reference/commands-and-permissions.md#access-groups) can grant
coins or monthly raffle tickets and run or review raffle draws.
[Core leadership](../../reference/commands-and-permissions.md#access-groups)
can review coin history, set raffle prizes, remove tickets, and reset draws.
Achievement awards can skip the public announcement with the `silent` option.

## Event trackers

Event trackers use channel names to show member counts or countdowns for
recurring and one-time events. `/event panel` opens the controls for creating
and managing them. `/event list` shows every tracker and its status.
`/event update` refreshes them immediately instead of waiting for the next
automatic update.

## Support tickets

`/open` creates a private support ticket for a member. `/close` locks
the ticket and saves its transcript; the closing controls can then reopen or
delete it.

## Hibernation

`/hibernate` saves a member's roles and moves them into hibernation.
`/reactivate` restores the saved roles and opens a return ticket. Previous rank
roles, such as Elder, aren't restored automatically.

## Role connections

`/connections` posts the board used to create and edit role rules. Each rule can
add or remove a role based on roles a member has or lacks.
